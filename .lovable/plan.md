

## Redesign "Who This Is For" Section

### What's Changing
Replace the current jarring "This is NOT for you" column with a gentler "Approaches That Don't Work" myth-busting format, based on your handwritten notes.

### New Section Layout

**Left column stays similar** — "This IS for you if" (keep as-is)

**Right column becomes "Why Other Approaches Don't Work"** with three myth-busting cards:

1. **"Set & forget" parental controls** — Kids find ways around these. Empowering safe use sets them up for the future.

2. **Banning all technology** — Unless you're moving to an off-grid location, this isn't realistic.

3. **Waiting for someone else to solve it** — Children learn from their parents and peers. Being a role model and setting up the entire family for safe, successful use of AI is what works.

### Design Changes
- Replace the `X` icons and red/destructive styling with a softer approach (e.g., `AlertCircle` or `BotOff` icons)
- Use a warm "myth vs reality" card style instead of the negative "not for you" list
- Section title changes from "The AI-Ready Families Framework Is For You If..." to something like **"Is This Right For You?"**
- Right column header: **"Why other approaches fall short"**
